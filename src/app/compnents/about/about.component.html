About Lolly’s Food Joint

Lolly’s Food Joint is a proudly local fast food brand built in the heart of Durban, serving bold, affordable, and irresistible street-style meals. From loaded kotas and crispy wings to classic gwinya and refreshing drinks, our food is made to satisfy real cravings.

We focus on quality, speed, and flavour, giving our customers the perfect balance between taste and value. Whether you’re grabbing a quick bite or ordering for friends and family, Lolly’s delivers food that keeps you coming back. At Lolly’s, it’s not just food — it’s a kasi experience with a modern twist.
